    Location in your photos

    On by default, Can be turned off
    • It is on by default. Turn it off in the app under Settings › Capture › Save Location in Photos, or set iOS Settings › OK Cam › Location to Never. New photos will then contain no location.
    • The location is stored inside the photo file; we never see it. If you send a photo to someone, the location goes with it. Tap “Options” at the top of the iOS share sheet to leave it out.

    Deleted with the app
    • Your RAW/JPG library, videos, edit settings, watermark and app settings.

    • If iCloud Backup is on, this data is included in your own iCloud backup, which Apple holds under its privacy policy. We cannot access it.
    • Deleting the app deletes this data. Photos and videos already saved to the Photos app are not affected.

    • यह डिफ़ॉल्ट रूप से चालू रहता है। इसे ऐप में Settings › Capture › Save Location in Photos से बंद करें, या iOS सेटिंग्ज़ › OK Cam › स्थान में “कभी नहीं” चुनें। बंद करने के बाद ली गई नई फ़ोटो में स्थान नहीं होगा।
